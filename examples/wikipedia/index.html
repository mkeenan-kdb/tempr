Every Wikimedia wiki reports each edit within seconds. Some edits are reverted, often within seconds or minutes. tempr counts edits per minute as they arrive; when a revert arrives, it corrects that edit, so a minute already in the past changes, and its bar flashes. Drag back to see the minutes exactly as they were known then.
